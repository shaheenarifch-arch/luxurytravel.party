Which jet fits your trip?

Light jets

4–7 passengers, best for trips up to about 2–3 hours, such as New York to Miami with a stop or Boston to Washington.

Midsize and super-midsize

7–9 passengers, more cabin and bag space, coast-to-coast with few or no stops.

Heavy jets

10–16 passengers, stand-up cabins and the range for transatlantic trips.

How to choose: start from the number of people and bags, then the longest leg. Not sure? Pick "Not sure" in the form and we'll suggest options.

Frequently asked questions

Do you own the aircraft?

No. LUX arranges charters as a broker: flights are operated by FAA-certificated charter operators that hold exclusive operational control. Your quote names the operator before you book.

How much does a private jet charter cost?

It depends on the aircraft size, flight time, positioning of the aircraft, airport fees and dates. A light jet for a short trip costs far less than a heavy jet across the country. Send the form for a written quote.

What is an empty leg?

A flight where the aircraft has to reposition without passengers. Operators often sell these one-way legs below the usual price. Times and routes are fixed by the operator, so they suit flexible travellers.

Can I bring my pet?

Usually yes, with the operator's approval. Tell us the type, size and number of pets in the request so we only quote aircraft that allow them.

How far ahead should I book?

A few days is often enough, and some trips can be arranged within 24 hours. Holidays and big events book up early, so ask as soon as you have dates.
