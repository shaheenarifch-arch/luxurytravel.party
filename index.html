Boat rental questions answered

What is the best way to rent a boat?

Search by destination, date, guests and boat type. Use discovery like LUX, then view live inventory on Sailo.

How much does it cost to rent a yacht?

Varies by size, season, location. Check live listing on Sailo for verified pricing.

Do I need a boating license?

Depends on location and captain. Many listings include captain option.

Can I rent a boat with a captain?

Yes, most Sailo listings offer captain-included.

Difference between yacht charter and boat rental?

Rental is short-term, charter is premium crewed.
