Boating knowledge

Boat rental questions answered

What is the best way to rent a boat?

Start with destination, guest count, and trip style. Then compare availability, pricing, and included amenities before booking.

Do I need a boating license?

Requirements vary by destination and charter type. Many rentals include a captain, while bareboat bookings may require a valid license or certification.

Can I rent a yacht for an event?

Yes. Many operators offer private charters for birthdays, proposals, sunset cruises, and small-group celebrations.
